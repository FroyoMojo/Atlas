# CodeRED: Orion: rules, tracks and judging

Copied from the [Devpost page](https://codered-orion-ccs.devpost.com/) and its [rules](https://codered-orion-ccs.devpost.com/rules) on Oct 10, 2026. Check the live page for changes.

## Key rules

- Hacking runs from **Oct 10, 1:00 pm to Oct 11, 1:00 pm CDT**. Submit on Devpost within that window.
- Teams of 1 to 4; current college students or graduates within one year.
- The project must be new, not created before the hackathon.
- **Submit the hosted website and the repository.**
- It must at least run on our own computer for the in-person demo.
- MLH rules also apply: [standard hackathon rules](https://github.com/MLH/mlh-policies/blob/main/standard-hackathon-rules.md).

## Judging criteria

| Criterion | What judges look for |
| --- | --- |
| Originality | Solves an unaddressed problem, or an old one in a novel way |
| Relevance to track | Meets the track's requirements and goes beyond them |
| Impact | Large problem, wide audience; the first choice for the people it targets |
| Demo | Smooth in-person demo; we understand the tech, the problem and the solution |
| Technical implementation | Well tested, complete for its scope, uses its stack well, few bugs |

## Tracks we are entering

| Track | Prize | What it asks for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best Overall | $1,000 | Most ambitious, high-impact, most complete and stable project |
| Best Pitch/Demo | $500 | Strongest business case: target users, why it beats alternatives, how it succeeds after the hackathon |
| Best Environment Hack | $500 | Technology for a healthier, more sustainable future: climate, conservation, pollution, protecting local communities |
| [MLH] Best Use of Solana | Ledger wallets | Fast, cheap, high-frequency transactions; trading, payments, identity or supply-chain prototypes |
| [MLH] Best Use of Tiger Data | Stream Deck Mini | Real-time data, time series, analytics; continuous aggregates, compression; financial prediction engines welcome |
| [MLH] Best Use of Vultr | M5Stack Tab5 | Cloud deploy and compute on Vultr |
| [MLH] Best Domain Name from GoDaddy Registry | Gift card | Register a domain through GoDaddy Registry |

Other tracks we are not targeting: ElevenLabs, Gemini API, Backboard, Best Hardware, Most Immersive, DEV Writing Challenge.
